Our Story & Heritage

North Star Bakery was founded with a simple goal: to bring honest, unhurried sourdough bread back to our local neighborhood. Long before the morning sun comes up, our team is hard at work feeding our natural sourdough starters, shaping loaves by hand, and firing up the ovens.

We believe that true flavor comes from patience. By allowing our dough to ferment naturally over 24 hours, we develop rich textures, deep aromas, and a crust that speaks for itself.

Visit Our Neighborhood Bakery

Located on Main Street, our cozy shop is designed to be a welcoming community gathering space. Stop by in the morning to catch the fresh aroma of warm bread coming straight from the oven.

Our Core Values

  • Simplicity & Purity: We rely exclusively on uncomplicated, unbleached flour, pure water, sea salt, and wild yeast. No artificial preservatives or chemical dough conditioners ever.
  • Regional Sourcing: We partner directly with regional grain farmers and local suppliers to ensure the freshest seasonal ingredients for our loaves and pastries.
  • Craftsmanship & Tradition: We honor traditional baking heritage, taking the extra time needed to hand-craft every single batch from scratch.
  • Community Warmth: Beyond baking bread, we strive to build a welcoming space where neighbors can meet, connect, and enjoy simple foods together.

Meet Our Master Bakers

Our dedicated baking team brings together decades of combined experience in classical French pastry making and traditional artisan sourdough fermentation. Every loaf that leaves our kitchen carries our commitment to quality and pride in our trade.
